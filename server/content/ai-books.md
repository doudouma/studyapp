## How we rank AI books

We score every book on three signals, then remove sponsored placements before
ranking. No publisher can pay for a position.

- Review volume (50%): review count, log-scaled. A book with 1,000 ratings is a
  stronger signal than one with 20.
- Rating quality (30%): a Bayesian-adjusted rating, so a 5.0 with 3 reviews does
  not outrank a 4.6 with 1,000.
- Title clarity (20%): penalizes keyword-stuffed and all-caps titles.

Ratings and review counts come from Amazon product pages and were captured on
2026-11-01.

## FAQ

### What is the best AI book for beginners?

Co-Intelligence by Ethan Mollick is the safest first read: short, practical and
written for non-specialists. If you want a hands-on path, pair it with AI for
Absolute Beginners or the free Google Machine Learning Crash Course.

### What is the best AI book for engineers?

AI Engineering by Chip Huyen is the best all-round pick for building with
foundation models. Go deeper with Designing Machine Learning Systems for
production ML, or Hands-On Machine Learning for classical ML and deep learning.

### How are these 84 AI books ranked?
