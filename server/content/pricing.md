# Pricing — 100mini

100mini is free to use. There is no paid checkout today: usage is metered in
in-app points, and membership is granted by the 100mini team (not self-serve).
Prices below are in points, an internal credit — not currency.

## Anonymous (no account)
- Price: $0
- Limits: 5 MB per page; pages auto-destroy after 7 days
- Not available: title/category/tags, sharing to the Learning Square, managing links
- Search: anonymous pages are noindex (not indexed by search engines)

## Free account
- Price: $0
- Starting points: 50
- Permanent pages: 5
- Content size: up to 50 MB per page
- Points used for:
  - Extra page beyond the 5 free slots: 10 points per upload
  - Custom link (/p/your-slug): 10 points each
  - Content over 5 MB: 10 points per 5 MB block
- Can add title/category/tags, share to the Learning Square, and manage all links

## Member
- Price: contact the team (granted, not self-serve)
- Permanent pages: unlimited
- Per-upload quota fee: none
- Size and custom-link point fees still apply

## Notes
- Points are an internal credit used to meter uploads; they are not purchased or refundable.
- Pages are stored on Cloudflare R2; metadata is stored in Cloudflare D1.
- Hosted pages run in a Content-Security-Policy sandbox with a phishing-prevention banner.
